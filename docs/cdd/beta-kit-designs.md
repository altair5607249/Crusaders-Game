# Beta Kit Designs
These are the final kit designs for the open beta of Crusaders! 

Future versions will receive stat adjustments and (potentially) entire reworks, but the main idea will remain the same for each character.

## Red
Also known as Aidalon, Red possesses an answer for everything in his kit! 

Though he's the roster's all-rounder, he also possesses a special gimmick: the lower his health gets, the stronger some of his moves become.
