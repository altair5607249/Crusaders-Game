# Dashing
This is the legendary tale of a single equation and the pure math, basic calculus, and pattern recognition behind it.

## Motion
I wanted to create an equation that would dictate a player character's displacement during a dash.

As easy as it would've been to find the equation I wanted online, I wanted to know if I was actually good at math. In my opinion, there's a huge difference between exceling at the curriculum and following your own path when it comes to math.
